In collaboration with the North Slope Borough Department of Wildlife Management, I am part of the team working on a 2025 census of the Bering-Chukchi-Beaufort (BCB) bowhead whale population offshore of Point Barrow, Alaska. I am the primary analyst responsible for acoustic localization within this project.

To optimize the placement of the hydrophones, I made a [user interface](https://bowhead-census-localization-evaluator.streamlit.app/) for evaluating localization coverage and accuracy for different array spacings and geometries. Through this visual and accessible approach, we co-designed the acoustic survey with our partners, balancing quantitative analysis metrics with prior experience and practical considerations for field deployment.

Our array of nine Rockhopper units was deployed, recovered, and recorded successfully. As of the fall of 2026, I am actively working on completing acoustic localization, for an updated population estimate in 2027.
